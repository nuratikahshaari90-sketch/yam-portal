# YAM Staff app: quick guide for supervisors and HR/Admin

Supervisors and HR also use everything in the staff guide (clocking in, leave, trips, claims).

## Who approves what (current setting)
| Item | Goes to |
|---|---|
| Leave | The staff member's **supervisor**, or **HR/Admin** if they have none |
| Outstation trips | The **supervisor**, or **HR/Admin** if none |
| Claims | **HR/Admin** |
| Overtime and attendance exceptions | **HR/Admin** |

HR can change this under **More → Settings → Who approves**. Changes apply to new requests only. Nobody can approve their own requests: HR's own leave and claims go to the **second HR/Admin** (e.g. the director).

## ✅ Approvals (supervisors and HR)
The red number on **Approvals** shows how many items are waiting for you. Filter by **Leave, Trips, Claims, Attend.** (attendance).
- **Approve**, or **Reject** with a comment (a comment is required to reject).
- Trips also have **Ask for changes**. The staff member sees your comment and can resubmit.
- Tap **View details** to see the full request, documents, and every receipt photo.

## Attendance exceptions (HR)
Entries appear under **Attend.** when they show:
- **Overtime**: worked hours above the daily threshold on a working day
- **Over daily hour limit**
- **Work on Off Day / Public Holiday**
- **Missed clock-out**
- **Outstation, no approved trip**
- **Recorded offline**

For a **missed clock-out**, enter the correct time and tap **Fix & approve**; the correction is logged. **Approve all shown** is available once you've checked the list. Rejected entries are left out of report totals.

## HR/Admin tools (More → HR/Admin)
- **Staff, roles & schedules:** add staff (name, work email, role), assign or change supervisors, and deactivate leavers (untick *Active*). **Change schedule** sets working days from a start date, e.g. Wed–Sun for weekend workers. Past records keep the schedule that applied at the time.
  *Changing a supervisor* only affects new requests. To move items already waiting, use **Reassign to another approver** on the approval card.
- **Public holidays:** keep the list up to date each year, including state holidays.
- **Settings:**
  - missed clock-out time (default 11:59 pm)
  - daily hour limit (10 h)
  - overtime threshold (8 h)
  - unpaid break (60 min, deducted when more than 5 h is worked)
  - minimum overtime (30 min)
  - mileage rate (RM per km)
  - who approves each item
- **Approvals → To pay:** approved claims waiting for payment. Enter the payment reference and tap **Mark paid**.
- **Sync issues:** rare cases where an offline entry clashed with another device. Nothing is lost: check it, correct the record if needed, then **Mark resolved**.

## 📊 Reports (supervisors see their team; HR sees everyone)
Choose the **Month**, then:
- **All staff:** days worked; days by work mode; total hours; overtime (approved and pending); Work on Off Day and Work on Public Holiday (days and hours); leave by type; missed clock-outs; working days with no record.
- **Per person:** summary plus a day-by-day table showing "On Leave (type)" and public holidays.
- **Claims:** claims submitted that month, by category, with totals and payment status.

Tap **⬇ Excel** or **⬇ PDF** to download. Hours are shown after the unpaid break.

## Good habits
Review **Approvals** at least twice a week, clear attendance exceptions before month-end, then download the month's reports for payroll and records.
