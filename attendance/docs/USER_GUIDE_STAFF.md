# YAM Staff app: quick guide for staff

**The app is built on trust.** There is no GPS tracking and no selfie. You simply type where you are.

## Getting started (once)
1. Open the link HR sent you. On **iPhone use Safari**; on **Android use Chrome**.
2. Tap **Activate your account**, enter your work email, and choose a password (at least 8 characters).
3. Open the email we send and tap the link. You're in.
4. Install it: **Android** ⋮ → *Install app*. **iPhone** Share (□↑) → *Add to Home Screen*.

Forgot your password? On the sign-in screen tap **Forgot password?**

## Every working day: ⏱ Clock
- **Clock In:** choose your work mode (Office, Work From Home, Client Site, Outstation, Training), type **where you are** (e.g. "Client office, Putrajaya") and **what you're working on**, then tap **Clock In**.
- **Clock Out:** optionally note what you got done, and a new location if you moved. Tap **Clock Out**.
- The time comes from the server, not your phone clock.
- Working on your **off day** or a **public holiday**? You can still clock in. It is tagged automatically so HR can see it.
- On **approved leave** you don't need to clock in.
- Forgot to clock out? Clock out as soon as you remember. HR will see it flagged and may correct the time.

## No signal? Keep going
Everything is saved on your phone first. The bar at the top shows **"Offline, X entries waiting to sync"**. It sends automatically when you're back online. Entries made offline keep your phone's time and are marked "recorded offline". **Don't sign out while entries are waiting.**

## 📅 History
See your entries by **Day, Week or Month**, with hours, overtime, leave days and anything awaiting HR review.

## 📝 Requests
- **Leave:** choose the type, start and end dates, full or half day, and a reason. Attach a document if you have one (e.g. an MC for Hospitalization Leave: tap 📷 to take a photo). The app shows how many **working days** it uses, based on your own schedule. Off days and public holidays don't count. **Save draft** works offline; **Submit** needs internet.
- **Outstation trip:** request it **before you travel**: destination, dates, purpose, and estimated cost (optional). If your approver asks for changes, open the trip, update it, and tap **Resubmit**.
  When you clock in as **Outstation**, the app links the entry to your approved trip. If there's no approved trip it warns you, and HR is alerted.
- **Claims** (for approved trips): add one item per expense (date, category, amount in RM, description, receipt photo). For **Mileage**, enter the kilometres; the app calculates the RM at HR's rate. Check the total, then **Submit claim**. Statuses: Draft → Submitted → Approved/Rejected → Paid.

## Status words
**Pending approval**: waiting for your approver. **Changes requested**: read the comment, edit, resubmit. **Approved / Rejected**: the decision, with any comment. **Awaiting HR review**: an attendance entry HR needs to check (e.g. overtime).

Questions? Contact HR.
